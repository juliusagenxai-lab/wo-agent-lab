Technology has changed how students learn in both positive and challenging ways. Students today have access to many tools that can help them understand subjects better and work more independently.

One positive effect is that students can access information quickly online. Instead of only using textbooks, students can search for articles, videos, and examples that explain topics in different ways. This can help students who learn best by seeing visuals or hearing explanations rather than reading.

A challenge with technology is that not every student has the same access. Some students have fast internet and new devices at home, while others do not. This creates an unequal situation where some students can do more research and practice outside of school.

In conclusion, technology influences learning in important ways. It can make learning more interesting and accessible, but schools and families need to work together to make sure all students have equal opportunities to benefit from it.
