Technology affects learning in several ways. Students can use computers and tablets to look up information quickly. Many schools use apps and websites to help students practice skills like reading and math. Technology makes it easier for students to find answers to questions.

One challenge with technology is that it can be distracting. Some students spend time on games or videos when they should be doing schoolwork. Teachers have to manage how students use devices to make sure they stay on task.

Another effect is that technology helps students who learn differently. For example, text-to-speech programs can help students who struggle with reading. Visual learners can watch videos to understand new ideas.

In conclusion, technology has both good and bad effects on learning. When students use it correctly, it can help them succeed. Schools need to teach students how to use technology responsibly.
