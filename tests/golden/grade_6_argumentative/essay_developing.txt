I believe students should NOT be allowed to use smartphones during the school day. Smartphones are a major distraction that prevents students from learning. When students use their phones in class, they miss important lessons and their grades suffer. Additionally, phones can be used for cyberbullying, which hurts other students. Schools should focus on education, not entertainment. For these reasons, smartphones should be banned during school hours. Students can use their phones before and after school when they are not supposed to be learning.
