Should students be allowed to use smartphones at school? I think students should not use phones during class because it distracts them from learning. Research shows that students who use phones during class get lower grades than students who don't.

Another reason is that phones can cause problems between students. When someone sends a mean message during school, it can ruin a person's whole day and make it hard to focus. Schools have a responsibility to keep students safe, and that includes online behavior during school hours.

Some students might say they need their phones for emergencies. That is a reasonable concern, but schools can solve this problem by allowing phones to be kept in lockers or turned in at the beginning of the day. Students can still reach their parents through the school office if something serious happens.

In conclusion, smartphones should not be used during the school day because they distract students and can cause social problems. Schools that limit phone use create better learning environments for everyone.
