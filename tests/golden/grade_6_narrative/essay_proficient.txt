One day I was walking home when I saw a strange door in the middle of the park. It was made of wood and had a golden handle. I was curious so I opened it.

On the other side was a beautiful forest with colorful plants and animals I had never seen before. The sky was purple and there were two moons. I walked in and looked around.

Suddenly a small creature ran up to me. It had big eyes and orange fur. "Welcome," it said. "You found the hidden world."

I was surprised that the creature could talk. It told me that this world needed help because a storm was coming that would destroy everything. I asked what I could do.

The creature said I needed to find a special crystal in the mountains. I agreed to help. We climbed the mountain together and found the crystal glowing in a cave. When I picked it up the storm clouds disappeared.

The creature thanked me and I walked back through the door. When I turned around the door was gone. I went home and thought about the adventure for the rest of the day.
