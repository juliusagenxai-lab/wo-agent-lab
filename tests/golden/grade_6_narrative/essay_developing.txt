I never expected to find a door behind the old bookcase in my grandmother's attic. It was small and painted green, with a round handle that felt warm when I touched it. Something about it made me feel like I was supposed to open it.

When I did, I stepped into a meadow covered in white flowers. The sky was a soft yellow and everything was very quiet. I walked forward carefully, not sure what to expect.

After a few minutes I heard a sound like bells and saw a small village in the distance. The houses were made of stone and had bright green roofs. When I got closer the villagers looked at me in surprise.

An old woman came forward and asked where I was from. When I told her she looked worried. She said no one from my world had come through the door in many years. She said I would need to find my way back before dark or the door would disappear.

I spent the afternoon looking for the door with the help of a boy named Eli. We found it just as the sun was starting to set. I said goodbye and stepped back through. The door closed behind me and when I turned around it was just a bookcase again.

I never told anyone what happened. But sometimes I look at old doors and wonder what might be on the other side.
