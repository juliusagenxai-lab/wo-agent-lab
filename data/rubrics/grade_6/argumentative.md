# Grade 6 Argumentative Writing Rubric

Standard W.6.1: Write arguments to support claims with clear reasons and relevant evidence.

## Criterion: Claim

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Clear, precise, and compelling claim that establishes a strong position on the topic |
| Proficient | 3 | Clear claim that establishes the writer's position on the topic |
| Developing | 2 | Claim is unclear, weak, or only partially establishes the writer's position |
| Beginning | 1 | No identifiable claim; position is absent or cannot be determined |

## Criterion: Evidence

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Relevant, specific, and well-developed evidence strongly supports the claim; sources or examples are precise |
| Proficient | 3 | Relevant supporting evidence is provided; examples or details adequately support the claim |
| Developing | 2 | Evidence is limited, insufficient, or only partially relevant to the claim |
| Beginning | 1 | Little or no evidence is provided; details do not support the claim |

## Criterion: Reasoning

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Strong logical explanation clearly connects evidence to the claim; reasoning is thorough and convincing |
| Proficient | 3 | Logical explanations connect evidence to the claim; reasoning is adequate |
| Developing | 2 | Some reasoning is present but inconsistent or unclear; connection between evidence and claim is weak |
| Beginning | 1 | Little or no reasoning; evidence is listed without explanation or connection to the claim |

## Criterion: Organization

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Excellent organizational structure with effective transitions; introduction, body, and conclusion are well-developed |
| Proficient | 3 | Logical organization with appropriate transitions; introduction, body, and conclusion are present |
| Developing | 2 | Inconsistent organization; transitions are limited or abrupt; structure is partially developed |
| Beginning | 1 | Disorganized; ideas are presented without clear structure or sequence |

## Criterion: Counterargument

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Effectively addresses and responds to opposing views; counterargument strengthens the writer's position |
| Proficient | 3 | Identifies an opposing view and provides a response |
| Developing | 2 | Weakly or vaguely addresses opposing views; response is insufficient |
| Beginning | 1 | No opposing view is acknowledged |

## Criterion: Language Conventions

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Very few or no errors in grammar, punctuation, spelling, or capitalization; writing demonstrates strong command of conventions |
| Proficient | 3 | Minor errors in conventions that do not affect meaning or readability |
| Developing | 2 | Frequent errors in conventions that sometimes affect meaning or readability |
| Beginning | 1 | Errors in conventions significantly affect meaning and readability |
