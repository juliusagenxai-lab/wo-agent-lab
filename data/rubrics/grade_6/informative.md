# Grade 6 Informative/Explanatory Writing Rubric

Standard W.6.2: Write informative/explanatory texts to examine a topic and convey ideas, concepts, and information.

## Criterion: Topic Introduction

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Engaging and clearly establishes the topic; hooks the reader and provides relevant background context |
| Proficient | 3 | Clearly introduces the topic; provides adequate context for the reader |
| Developing | 2 | Introduction is vague or only partially introduces the topic |
| Beginning | 1 | No clear introduction; topic is absent or cannot be identified |

## Criterion: Development of Ideas

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Thorough and insightful explanation with relevant, well-chosen details that fully develop the topic |
| Proficient | 3 | Adequate explanation with supporting details that develop the topic |
| Developing | 2 | Limited explanation; ideas are present but not fully developed |
| Beginning | 1 | Ideas are undeveloped; explanation is absent or minimal |

## Criterion: Facts and Examples

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Specific, accurate, and relevant facts, definitions, details, and examples strongly support the explanation |
| Proficient | 3 | Relevant facts and examples are provided to support the explanation |
| Developing | 2 | Few or weak examples; facts are limited or only partially relevant |
| Beginning | 1 | No meaningful supporting details, facts, or examples |

## Criterion: Organization

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Logical and cohesive structure with effective transitions that connect ideas across paragraphs |
| Proficient | 3 | Logical paragraph structure with appropriate transitions |
| Developing | 2 | Some organizational problems; transitions are limited or unclear |
| Beginning | 1 | Ideas lack organization; paragraphs are absent or randomly ordered |

## Criterion: Conclusion

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Strong conclusion that reinforces key information and provides a sense of closure; may offer a final insight |
| Proficient | 3 | Appropriate conclusion that relates to the information presented |
| Developing | 2 | Weak or repetitive conclusion; closure is limited or abrupt |
| Beginning | 1 | Conclusion is missing or unrelated to the topic |

## Criterion: Language Conventions

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Very few or no errors in grammar, punctuation, spelling, or capitalization; writing demonstrates strong command of conventions |
| Proficient | 3 | Minor errors in conventions that do not affect meaning or readability |
| Developing | 2 | Frequent errors in conventions that sometimes affect meaning or readability |
| Beginning | 1 | Errors in conventions significantly interfere with understanding |
