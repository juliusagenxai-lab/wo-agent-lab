# Grade 6 Narrative Writing Rubric

Standard W.6.3: Write narratives to develop real or imagined experiences or events using effective technique, relevant descriptive details, and well-structured event sequences.

## Criterion: Plot Development

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Creative, engaging, and well-developed plot with a clear conflict, rising action, climax, and resolution |
| Proficient | 3 | Clear and logical sequence of events with a recognizable problem and resolution |
| Developing | 2 | Events are partially developed; plot structure is incomplete or unclear |
| Beginning | 1 | Events are unclear or missing; no recognizable narrative structure |

## Criterion: Characters

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Well-developed characters with clear motivations, personalities, and growth throughout the narrative |
| Proficient | 3 | Characters are identifiable and have basic traits or motivations |
| Developing | 2 | Limited character development; characters lack detail or motivation |
| Beginning | 1 | Characters are unclear, absent, or undifferentiated |

## Criterion: Setting

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Vivid and detailed setting that immerses the reader; time and place are clearly and creatively established |
| Proficient | 3 | Setting is clearly established; time and place are identifiable |
| Developing | 2 | Setting has limited detail; time or place is vague |
| Beginning | 1 | Setting is missing or unclear; reader cannot determine time or place |

## Criterion: Organization

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Effective and purposeful beginning, middle, and ending; pacing and sequencing enhance the narrative |
| Proficient | 3 | Logical sequence of events with identifiable beginning, middle, and ending |
| Developing | 2 | Some sequencing problems; beginning, middle, or ending is weak or missing |
| Beginning | 1 | Disorganized sequence; narrative lacks structure |

## Criterion: Descriptive Language

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Strong sensory details, vivid word choices, and effective dialogue bring the narrative to life |
| Proficient | 3 | Appropriate descriptive details and some dialogue support the narrative |
| Developing | 2 | Limited descriptive language; details are sparse or generic |
| Beginning | 1 | Little or no descriptive language; writing is flat or unclear |

## Criterion: Language Conventions

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Very few or no errors in grammar, punctuation, spelling, or capitalization; writing demonstrates strong command of conventions |
| Proficient | 3 | Minor errors in conventions that do not affect meaning or readability |
| Developing | 2 | Frequent errors in conventions that sometimes affect meaning or readability |
| Beginning | 1 | Errors in conventions significantly interfere with meaning |
