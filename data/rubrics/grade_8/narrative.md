# Grade 8 Narrative Writing Rubric

Standard W.8.3: Write narratives to develop real or imagined experiences or events using effective technique, relevant descriptive details, and well-structured event sequences.

## Criterion: Plot Development

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Sophisticated and compelling plot with skillful development of conflict, tension, and resolution; advanced narrative techniques (e.g., nonlinear structure, foreshadowing, irony) are used purposefully and effectively |
| Proficient | 3 | Well-developed plot with a clear conflict, rising action, and resolution; narrative techniques are used competently |
| Developing | 2 | Plot is present but unevenly developed; conflict or resolution lacks detail; narrative techniques are attempted but inconsistently effective |
| Beginning | 1 | Plot is absent or unclear; no recognizable narrative structure |

## Criterion: Characters

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Fully realized characters with distinct voices, complex motivations, and meaningful development that contributes to the theme or central conflict |
| Proficient | 3 | Characters are well-defined with clear motivations and consistent traits that support the narrative |
| Developing | 2 | Characters are present but lack depth, development, or consistency |
| Beginning | 1 | Characters are absent, undifferentiated, or unclear |

## Criterion: Setting

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Richly detailed setting that is purposefully integrated into the narrative; sensory language creates a vivid atmosphere that contributes to mood, theme, or character development |
| Proficient | 3 | Setting is clearly established with specific details; time and place contribute to the narrative |
| Developing | 2 | Setting is mentioned but lacks specific detail or integration into the narrative |
| Beginning | 1 | Setting is absent or cannot be determined |

## Criterion: Organization

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Purposeful and masterful narrative structure; pacing, sequencing, and transitions are varied and precise, creating a compelling and unified reading experience |
| Proficient | 3 | Logical and coherent sequence of events with effective transitions; beginning, middle, and ending are well-developed |
| Developing | 2 | Sequencing problems affect readability; structural elements are unevenly developed; transitions are weak |
| Beginning | 1 | Disorganized sequence; narrative lacks structure |

## Criterion: Descriptive Language

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Precise, sophisticated word choices, varied sentence structures, and vivid sensory details create a rich narrative experience; dialogue is purposeful, natural, and reveals character |
| Proficient | 3 | Effective descriptive language and varied word choices support the narrative; dialogue is used appropriately |
| Developing | 2 | Descriptive language is present but limited or generic; sentence structure is repetitive |
| Beginning | 1 | Little or no descriptive language; writing is flat, unclear, or difficult to follow |

## Criterion: Language Conventions

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Very few or no errors; demonstrates sophisticated command of conventions; uses punctuation (e.g., dashes, ellipses) intentionally to convey meaning |
| Proficient | 3 | Minor errors in conventions that do not affect meaning or readability |
| Developing | 2 | Frequent errors that sometimes affect meaning or readability |
| Beginning | 1 | Errors significantly interfere with meaning |
