# Grade 8 Argumentative Writing Rubric

Standard W.8.1: Write arguments to support claims with clear reasons and relevant evidence, using credible sources and acknowledging and distinguishing claims from alternate or opposing claims.

## Criterion: Claim

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Precise, compelling, and nuanced claim clearly establishes the writer's position, anticipates complexity, and distinguishes the claim from alternate or opposing claims |
| Proficient | 3 | Clear and specific claim establishes the writer's position and is distinguishable from alternate claims |
| Developing | 2 | Claim is present but imprecise, overly broad, or not clearly distinguished from other claims |
| Beginning | 1 | No identifiable claim; position is absent or cannot be determined |

## Criterion: Evidence

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Relevant, accurate, and well-chosen evidence from multiple credible sources is skillfully integrated and cited; evidence directly and convincingly supports the claim |
| Proficient | 3 | Relevant evidence from credible sources supports the claim; evidence is adequately integrated |
| Developing | 2 | Evidence is present but limited, insufficiently credible, or only partially relevant to the claim |
| Beginning | 1 | Little or no evidence; details do not support the claim |

## Criterion: Reasoning

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Sophisticated and logically rigorous reasoning demonstrates a thorough understanding of the relationship between evidence and claim; explanations show depth of analysis |
| Proficient | 3 | Logical reasoning adequately explains the connection between evidence and claim |
| Developing | 2 | Some reasoning is present but inconsistent, superficial, or incomplete |
| Beginning | 1 | Little or no reasoning; evidence is stated without explanation or analysis |

## Criterion: Organization

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Sophisticated organizational structure with varied, precise transitions that create a unified and compelling argument; all structural elements are fully and effectively developed |
| Proficient | 3 | Logical and coherent organization with effective transitions; introduction, body, and conclusion are well-developed |
| Developing | 2 | Inconsistent organization; structural elements are present but unevenly developed; transitions are weak |
| Beginning | 1 | Disorganized; ideas lack clear structure |

## Criterion: Counterargument

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Thoroughly and effectively addresses alternate or opposing claims using logical reasoning and evidence; rebuttal is persuasive and significantly strengthens the argument |
| Proficient | 3 | Acknowledges alternate or opposing claims and provides a logical response |
| Developing | 2 | Opposing claim is acknowledged but response is weak, underdeveloped, or unconvincing |
| Beginning | 1 | No alternate or opposing claim is acknowledged |

## Criterion: Language Conventions

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Very few or no errors; demonstrates sophisticated command of language conventions; uses precise, varied vocabulary appropriate to Grade 8 argument writing |
| Proficient | 3 | Minor errors in conventions that do not affect meaning or readability; vocabulary is appropriate |
| Developing | 2 | Frequent errors that sometimes affect meaning or readability |
| Beginning | 1 | Errors significantly affect meaning and readability |
