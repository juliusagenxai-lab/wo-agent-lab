# Grade 8 Informative/Explanatory Writing Rubric

Standard W.8.2: Write informative/explanatory texts to examine a topic and convey ideas, concepts, and information through the selection, organization, and analysis of relevant content.

## Criterion: Topic Introduction

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Sophisticated introduction that precisely establishes the topic, previews the organizational structure, and immediately engages the reader with a compelling hook |
| Proficient | 3 | Introduction clearly establishes the topic and provides relevant context; previews what is to follow |
| Developing | 2 | Introduction is present but underdeveloped, vague, or does not adequately establish the topic |
| Beginning | 1 | No clear introduction; topic cannot be identified |

## Criterion: Development of Ideas

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Ideas are developed with precision and depth; the writer selects, organizes, and analyzes content to demonstrate thorough understanding of the topic |
| Proficient | 3 | Ideas are adequately developed with relevant facts, definitions, and analysis |
| Developing | 2 | Development is present but superficial; ideas lack sufficient explanation or analysis |
| Beginning | 1 | Ideas are absent or minimally present |

## Criterion: Facts and Examples

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Carefully selected, accurate, and well-integrated facts, concrete details, quotations, and examples directly and convincingly support the explanation |
| Proficient | 3 | Relevant facts and examples support the explanation adequately |
| Developing | 2 | Facts or examples are limited, vague, or insufficiently integrated |
| Beginning | 1 | No meaningful supporting details, facts, or examples |

## Criterion: Organization

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Sophisticated organizational structure appropriate to the task with varied, precise transitions that create cohesion and clarify complex relationships among ideas |
| Proficient | 3 | Logical paragraph structure with varied transitions that connect ideas and aid comprehension |
| Developing | 2 | Organizational problems are present; transitions are weak, repetitive, or missing |
| Beginning | 1 | Ideas lack organization; paragraphs are absent or randomly ordered |

## Criterion: Conclusion

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Sophisticated conclusion that follows from and reflects on the information presented; offers insight, a call to action, or a broader implication beyond the essay |
| Proficient | 3 | Conclusion follows from the information and provides appropriate closure |
| Developing | 2 | Conclusion is weak, repetitive, or does not connect meaningfully to the essay |
| Beginning | 1 | Conclusion is missing or unrelated to the information presented |

## Criterion: Language Conventions

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Very few or no errors; demonstrates sophisticated command of conventions; uses domain-specific vocabulary precisely and consistently |
| Proficient | 3 | Minor errors in conventions that do not affect meaning or readability; domain-specific vocabulary is used appropriately |
| Developing | 2 | Frequent errors that sometimes affect meaning or readability |
| Beginning | 1 | Errors significantly interfere with understanding |
