# Grade 7 Argumentative Writing Rubric

Standard W.7.1: Write arguments to support claims with clear reasons and relevant evidence, acknowledging alternate or opposing claims.

## Criterion: Claim

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Precise and compelling claim clearly establishes the writer's position and anticipates the complexity of the topic |
| Proficient | 3 | Clear and specific claim establishes the writer's position on the topic |
| Developing | 2 | Claim is present but lacks precision or only partially addresses the topic |
| Beginning | 1 | No identifiable claim; position is absent or cannot be determined |

## Criterion: Evidence

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Relevant, accurate, and well-chosen evidence from multiple credible sources strongly supports the claim; evidence is integrated smoothly |
| Proficient | 3 | Relevant evidence from one or more sources supports the claim adequately |
| Developing | 2 | Evidence is present but limited, insufficient, or only partially relevant |
| Beginning | 1 | Little or no evidence; details do not support the claim |

## Criterion: Reasoning

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Thorough and insightful reasoning clearly and logically connects evidence to the claim; explanations demonstrate sophisticated thinking |
| Proficient | 3 | Logical reasoning connects evidence to the claim; explanations are adequate |
| Developing | 2 | Some reasoning is present but inconsistent; connection between evidence and claim is underdeveloped |
| Beginning | 1 | Little or no reasoning; evidence is stated without explanation |

## Criterion: Organization

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Sophisticated organizational structure with varied and effective transitions; introduction, body paragraphs, and conclusion are fully developed and cohesive |
| Proficient | 3 | Logical organization with appropriate transitions; introduction, body, and conclusion are present and functional |
| Developing | 2 | Inconsistent organization; transitions are limited or abrupt; some structural elements are weak or missing |
| Beginning | 1 | Disorganized; ideas lack clear structure or sequence |

## Criterion: Counterargument

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Thoroughly acknowledges and responds to opposing or alternate claims; rebuttal is logical and strengthens the writer's position |
| Proficient | 3 | Acknowledges an opposing or alternate claim and provides a response |
| Developing | 2 | Opposing claim is mentioned but response is weak, vague, or insufficient |
| Beginning | 1 | No opposing or alternate claim is acknowledged |

## Criterion: Language Conventions

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Very few or no errors in grammar, punctuation, spelling, or capitalization; demonstrates strong command of language conventions appropriate for Grade 7 |
| Proficient | 3 | Minor errors in conventions that do not affect meaning or readability |
| Developing | 2 | Frequent errors that sometimes affect meaning or readability |
| Beginning | 1 | Errors significantly affect meaning and readability |
