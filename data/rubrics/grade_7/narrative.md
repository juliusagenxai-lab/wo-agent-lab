# Grade 7 Narrative Writing Rubric

Standard W.7.3: Write narratives to develop real or imagined experiences or events using effective technique, relevant descriptive details, and well-structured event sequences.

## Criterion: Plot Development

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Engaging and sophisticated plot with a well-developed conflict, effective pacing, and a satisfying resolution; narrative techniques (e.g., foreshadowing, flashback) are used purposefully |
| Proficient | 3 | Clear and logical sequence of events with a recognizable conflict and resolution; narrative techniques are used adequately |
| Developing | 2 | Plot structure is present but incomplete; conflict or resolution may be weak or underdeveloped |
| Beginning | 1 | Events are unclear or absent; no recognizable narrative structure |

## Criterion: Characters

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Fully developed characters with distinct voices, clear motivations, and meaningful development over the course of the narrative |
| Proficient | 3 | Characters are clearly identifiable with basic traits or motivations that drive the narrative |
| Developing | 2 | Characters are present but lack development, motivation, or distinctiveness |
| Beginning | 1 | Characters are absent, unclear, or undifferentiated |

## Criterion: Setting

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Vivid and immersive setting with specific sensory details that contribute meaningfully to mood, tone, or plot |
| Proficient | 3 | Setting is clearly established with adequate descriptive details; time and place are identifiable |
| Developing | 2 | Setting is mentioned but lacks detail or does not contribute to the narrative |
| Beginning | 1 | Setting is absent or cannot be determined from the text |

## Criterion: Organization

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Purposeful and effective narrative structure; pacing, sequencing, and transitions enhance the story and create a satisfying experience |
| Proficient | 3 | Logical sequence of events with identifiable beginning, middle, and ending; transitions are adequate |
| Developing | 2 | Some sequencing problems; beginning, middle, or ending is weak or missing; transitions are limited |
| Beginning | 1 | Disorganized sequence; narrative lacks structure |

## Criterion: Descriptive Language

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Precise, vivid word choices and strong sensory details create a compelling narrative experience; dialogue, if used, is natural and purposeful |
| Proficient | 3 | Appropriate descriptive language and details support the narrative; dialogue, if used, is functional |
| Developing | 2 | Descriptive language is limited or generic; details are sparse and do not enhance the narrative |
| Beginning | 1 | Little or no descriptive language; writing is flat or unclear |

## Criterion: Language Conventions

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Very few or no errors in grammar, punctuation, spelling, or capitalization; demonstrates strong command of conventions |
| Proficient | 3 | Minor errors in conventions that do not affect meaning or readability |
| Developing | 2 | Frequent errors that sometimes affect meaning or readability |
| Beginning | 1 | Errors significantly interfere with meaning |
