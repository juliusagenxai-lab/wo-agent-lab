# Grade 7 Informative/Explanatory Writing Rubric

Standard W.7.2: Write informative/explanatory texts to examine a topic and convey ideas, concepts, and information through the selection, organization, and analysis of relevant content.

## Criterion: Topic Introduction

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Engaging introduction clearly establishes the topic, previews what is to follow, and draws the reader in with a strong hook or relevant context |
| Proficient | 3 | Introduction clearly introduces the topic and provides adequate context for the reader |
| Developing | 2 | Introduction is present but vague, underdeveloped, or only partially introduces the topic |
| Beginning | 1 | No clear introduction; topic cannot be identified |

## Criterion: Development of Ideas

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Ideas are thoroughly and insightfully developed with carefully chosen facts, definitions, concrete details, and quotations that go beyond surface-level explanation |
| Proficient | 3 | Ideas are adequately developed with relevant facts, definitions, and details |
| Developing | 2 | Ideas are present but underdeveloped; explanation lacks sufficient detail or analysis |
| Beginning | 1 | Ideas are absent or minimally developed |

## Criterion: Facts and Examples

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Specific, accurate, and well-chosen facts, examples, and quotations are seamlessly integrated and directly support the explanation |
| Proficient | 3 | Relevant facts and examples are provided and adequately support the explanation |
| Developing | 2 | Facts or examples are limited, vague, or only partially relevant |
| Beginning | 1 | No meaningful supporting details, facts, or examples |

## Criterion: Organization

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Sophisticated and logical structure with varied, effective transitions that create cohesion and clarify relationships among ideas |
| Proficient | 3 | Logical paragraph structure with appropriate transitions that connect ideas |
| Developing | 2 | Some organizational problems; transitions are limited, repetitive, or unclear |
| Beginning | 1 | Ideas lack organization; paragraphs are absent or randomly ordered |

## Criterion: Conclusion

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Strong conclusion that follows logically from the information presented, reinforces key ideas, and provides closure or a broader perspective |
| Proficient | 3 | Conclusion relates to the information presented and provides appropriate closure |
| Developing | 2 | Conclusion is weak, repetitive, or abrupt; closure is limited |
| Beginning | 1 | Conclusion is missing or unrelated to the information presented |

## Criterion: Language Conventions

| Level | Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Advanced | 4 | Very few or no errors in grammar, punctuation, spelling, or capitalization; demonstrates strong command of conventions and uses domain-specific vocabulary effectively |
| Proficient | 3 | Minor errors in conventions that do not affect meaning or readability |
| Developing | 2 | Frequent errors that sometimes affect meaning or readability |
| Beginning | 1 | Errors significantly interfere with understanding |
